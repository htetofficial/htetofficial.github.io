Nara Mini v1 is adapted from Kokoro for Burmese and English speech synthesis.
Kokoro is published by hexgrad under the Apache License, Version 2.0.
https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M

The four preset voices were developed using synthetic speech generated with
VoxCPM2, published by OpenBMB under the Apache License, Version 2.0.
https://huggingface.co/openbmb/VoxCPM2

Nara adaptations include Burmese pronunciation processing, number verbalization,
voice adaptation, and mobile ONNX export with selective decoder quantization.

Modifications in Htet Official Digital Archive (2026):
- Ported Burmese G2P, number verbalization, and lexicon lookup rules to JavaScript (js/nara-g2p.js, data/nara-lexicon.json) for opt-in browser ONNX Runtime Web synthesis (js/nara-tts.js).
- Added Method 1 batch generator helper (scripts/generate_nara_audio.py) and Apache 2.0 attribution notices.
